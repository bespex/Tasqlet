## Keys, drop and a command line

- **Global key** to show and hide the popover, recorded in the settings (X11). On Wayland, bind
  `tasqlet --toggle` in your desktop.
- **Keys in the popover**: `Ctrl+1/2/3` focus, ideas, delegated; `Ctrl+Tab` cycles; `Ctrl+P` coffee
  break; `Ctrl+O` opens all resources; `Ctrl+L` back to the quick entry. Tooltips name their keys.
- **Drop** files, folders and links onto a task, line, idea or the elaboration: they become its
  resources. One `Ctrl+Z` takes the whole drop back. Paths with spaces work.
- **Pin** keeps the popover open when it loses focus, handy for dragging from the file manager.
- **Command line**: `tasqlet add "text"` (with `--now`, `--inter`, `--idea`, `--link`), `tasqlet open 1`,
  `tasqlet current`, `tasqlet list` (both with `--json`). Works with or without the app running.
- Empty resource and checklist sections take only one line.
