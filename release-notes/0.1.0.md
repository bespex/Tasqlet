## The first release

Tasqlet lives in your tray and keeps one task in focus. Organise, don't monitor: no time tracking, no
deadlines, no reminders.

- **Quick entry** with live recognition: `Ctrl ⇧ ⏎` now, `Ctrl ⏎` focus, `Alt ⏎` in between, `⏎` idea.
  `@Name` delegates, `!date` notes a day, links, paths and mails become resources. Jira projects are
  learned from links, so a bare key like `NMS-123` becomes a link.
- **Work card** for the current task: round ✓, checklist, note that saves while you type, coffee break.
- **Queue** of what is urgent: keyboard (`↑`/`↓`, `Enter`, `Alt+↑/↓`, `Alt+Home`) and drag at the grip.
- **Resources** open with one click or `Alt+1` … `Alt+9`, also from the tray menu.
- **In between**: small things as pills at the bottom; click takes over, middle click ticks off.
- **Ideas** grow with brainstorm lines; **Delegated** is grouped by person.
- **Search** (`Ctrl+F`) over everything, finished items included.
- **Elaboration** (`Ctrl+E`): a roomy window with all items on the left and a full editor on the right.
- **Settings**: language (German, English), popover position, autostart at login, forget Jira projects.
- Undo for every step (`Ctrl+Z`), local data in `~/.local/share/tasqlet` with backups.
