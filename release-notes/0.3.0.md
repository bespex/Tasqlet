## Popover or sidebar, themes and a calmer frame

- **Popover or sidebar**: under *Settings → General → Open as* Tasqlet opens either as a popover at the
  tray icon or as a sidebar over the full height of the screen, on the side of the tray icon or an edge
  you pick. The choice holds for the tray click, the global key and `tasqlet --toggle` alike. The
  sidebar stays when you click elsewhere and is kept on top only when pinned (📌); the key then brings
  it to the front instead of hiding it.
- **Colour themes and styles** from the be spex kit, the same choice as in every be spex app: twelve
  colour themes and three styles plus your own, under *Settings → Theme*.
- **Settings in a window of their own**: the popover stays open meanwhile and shows every change at
  once. Same layout as Maillet's settings.
- **A wider popover** (700 px) with the app mark in the header, a status bar at the bottom (read-only,
  coffee break) and a "Next" heading above the queue.
- **Faster with a long history**: done items no longer travel to the window on every change, and undo
  no longer copies all items for every step.
- Checklist boxes keep their size next to long step text.
